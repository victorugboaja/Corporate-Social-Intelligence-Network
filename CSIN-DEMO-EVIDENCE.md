# CSIN Canada-only evidence packet — revision 3

All organizations, locations, requests and outcomes are sample demo fixtures. This packet replaces the earlier international packet. CSIN means Corporate Social Intelligence Network. Discuss Canadian community investment only.

## Ottawa Access Collective

ID: ottawa. Location: Ottawa, Ontario, Canada. Sample request: CAD 20000. Program: Mentoring.

Employment mentoring for residents facing barriers to professional networks.

### O1-S1: Initial program report — synthetic

Our pilot enrolled 40 participants. Thirty completed the mentoring program. Twelve participants told us they had started paid work within three months of completion. We have not obtained employer confirmation. The program spent CAD 18,000: CAD 12,000 on delivery staff, CAD 4,000 on participant travel and access support, and CAD 2,000 on administration. We request CAD 20,000 to run the next cohort.

### O1-S2: Follow-up update — synthetic

We contacted the twelve participants who previously reported starting work. Nine responded and reconfirmed that they had started paid work; three did not respond. No employer evidence was collected. We have separately recorded four new self-reported job starts among other program completers. These observations do not establish that the mentoring caused employment.

Evidence gaps: Independent evidence of employment outcomes; Follow-up coverage and measurement approach; A budget for the next cohort.

## Vancouver Skills Circle

ID: vancouver. Location: Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. Sample request: CAD 15000. Program: Mentoring.

Peer mentoring and professional introductions for people with international experience.

### O2-CA-S1: Project brief — synthetic

We hosted six professional-networking sessions with 55 unique attendees. Eighteen attendees opted into peer mentoring. We have collected satisfaction forms but do not yet track employment outcomes. We request CAD 15,000 to improve accessible meeting space and outcome follow-up.

Evidence gaps: Program budget and cost detail; Employment follow-up measures; Detailed next-cohort budget.

## Prairie Pathways Society

ID: calgary. Location: Calgary, Alberta, Canada. Sample request: CAD 80000. Program: Training.

A sample employment training and employer-introduction program for young adults.

### O4-S1: Pilot brief (synthetic) — synthetic

Our Calgary pilot enrolled 24 participants in skills training. Eighteen completed the workshops. Seven participants reported starting paid work within two months; these reports have not been independently verified. We request CAD 80,000 for the proposed next cohort. The proposed milestone is to publish a follow-up summary after the next cohort finishes; it has not yet been achieved.

Evidence gaps: Independent follow-up evidence; Detailed CAD 80,000 budget; Delivery dates for the proposed next cohort.

## Interpretation rules

Do not double-count follow-up confirmations as new job starts. Ottawa’s initial 12 self-reports include 9 later reconfirmations and 3 non-responses; 4 additional starts are separate self-reports. None is independently verified. A human approving a finding does not independently verify its source. Reported activity is not proof of outcomes. No causal impact claim is supported.

The app may pass no live context: ask which organization and version, and do not claim to see review notes, authorization or payment status unless explicitly supplied. No authority to approve, pay or sign. Devnet SOL is valueless test currency. A devnet receipt does not pay the CAD request or prove impact.
