# Map attribution

Canada provincial/territorial shapes derive from Natural Earth via BenPortner/geojson-atlas:
https://github.com/BenPortner/geojson-atlas/blob/main/geojson/natural_earth/countries/10m/CA.geojson

CC0. Generalized and projected locally for the demo. Not navigation or legal boundary data. Organization coordinates represent city-level fictional demo locations, not real premises.
