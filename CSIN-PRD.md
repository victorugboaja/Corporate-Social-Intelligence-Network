# CSIN — Corporate Social Intelligence Network

Updated scope, 26 September 2026. Supersedes the Canada–Europe/three-profile version. CEPI is a separate project.

## Purpose

Help corporate funders discover Canadian nonprofits and assess the evidence supporting their funding requests. AI assists with explanation; humans review findings and authorize test funding. The hackathon uses four conspicuously sample organizations and synthetic sources.

## Locked journey

Landing → Scout → Assess → Review → Fund → Track → New evidence → Review again.

The actual Lovable landing supplies the layout/components. A miniature animated Canada network previews the full Scout experience. Both landing calls to action lead to /scout. No recreation of a screenshot is needed now that source has been supplied.

## Requirements

- Scout: Canada map and prominent scanning lens; filter curated fixtures by region, focus, funding range, program type and available evidence. Explain empty results without claiming no real charities exist.
- Assess: source excerpts, reported outcomes, budgets, funding request, gaps and milestones. No inference of independently verified impact from self-reports or human approval.
- Analyst: existing ElevenLabs AI agent, voice beside evidence. User starts/ends the conversation; supplied knowledge only. Local reviews are not automatically shared. Voice-controlled scouting and synchronized highlighting are stretch work, not promised.
- Review: approve, correct or flag findings, with notes for flags/corrections. Updated evidence reopens the affected review and preserves earlier report history/exports.
- Fund: all current findings reviewed; explicit separate authorization; browser wallet signature; 0.001 native devnet SOL plus fee. Show sender, recipient and network. Verify actual successful transfer details and persist a receipt. Never mark the sample CAD grant paid. Unknown status stays unresolved; never invent a signature or retry blindly.
- Track: local portfolio of reviewed cases and receipts, with report-version links and Ottawa's supplied follow-up.
- Deploy: working HTTPS Node deployment and final-origin checks. Vultr/domain are no longer blockers to the core demo; hosting and any paid purchase require concrete setup choices. No deployed service is claimed yet.

## Boundaries

Canada only. No live crawling, ranking of social value, real nonprofit onboarding, production accounts, real grants, payment custody, mainnet, RTR, exchange, custom blockchain program or automatic payouts. A funding receipt does not prove social impact. Seeded reports are labelled prepared content.

## Acceptance

Walk from landing to Scout to an evidence source; demonstrate a flagged finding and updated finding needing review; export reports; hold one live spoken analyst conversation; perform and verify one devnet transfer with explicit human authorization; show receipt and new evidence in Track. Test on deployed HTTPS. Sponsor eligibility is not established by implementation alone.
